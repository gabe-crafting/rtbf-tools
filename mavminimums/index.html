Mavminimums

The Pixhawk/Mavlink/Mavproxy/Ardupilot Telemetry App provides a simple, real-time overview of the essential information from a Pixhawk flight controller. It is designed to show the most important telemetry at a glance, without the complexity of a full ground-control system.

It displays key information including battery level and predicted remaining battery life, armed/disarmed state, altitude, Return-to-Home status, GPS status, and other fundamental flight data. The interface is deliberately simple, providing a quick, clear snapshot of the aircraft’s current state.
